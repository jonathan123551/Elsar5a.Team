# Homepage theatre scenes

- `curtain/closed-velvet.webp` is the photographed velvet surface used for the closed-curtain overture. The opening motion pulls this material into two weighted wings.
- `theatre/empty-stage.webp` is the empty auditorium plate used underneath the curtain and through the forward camera journey. It intentionally contains no team identity or production poster; those stay bound to the real repository logo and application data.

Both images are optimized WebP scene plates. They are visual scenery only and never supply show or booking information.
